TapPass – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026 · App: TapPass: Loyalty Card Wallet (Android, package com.cubemobilegames.tappass)

In short: your loyalty cards, shopping list and history stay on your phone. TapPass has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we never sell your data. If you turn on Store alerts, your location is used to notice when you arrive at a store whose card you saved – including when the app is closed – and only for that purpose.

1. Who we are

TapPass is developed and published by Cube Mobile Games (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question or request, contact us at cubemobilegame@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The following data is created by you and stored only on your device. We do not upload it to our servers, and we do not have access to it:

  • Loyalty cards – store name, card number or code, code format (e.g. barcode or QR code) and the colour you chose.
  • Shopping list – the items you add.
  • Activity history – when a card was shown, added or removed, and store alerts you received.
  • App settings – e.g. theme, Store alerts on/off.

You can delete this data at any time in Settings (“Delete All Cards”, “Clear Activity History”) or by uninstalling the app. Depending on your Android settings, it may be included in your device’s own Google backup, which is controlled by you and Google.

3. Location (Store alerts)

Store alerts is an optional TapPass Pro feature and is off until you turn it on. Before Android asks for permission, TapPass explains how location is used.

  • What we use: your device’s precise location, including in the background (when the app is closed or not in use), because the alert must appear when you arrive at a store, not only while the app is open.
  • Why: only to detect that you are near a store of a chain whose loyalty card you saved, and to show a notification that opens that card.
  • Where it is processed: on your device. TapPass compares your position with a store list built into the app. Your location history is not stored and is not sent to us.
  • Online store lookup: for chains that are not in the built-in list, the app may send your approximate position and the names of those chains to a public map-data service (the Overpass API for OpenStreetMap data) to find nearby stores. That service also sees your IP address, as with any internet request. No name, account or device identifier is sent.
  • While active: Android shows a persistent notification (“Watching for your stores nearby”) as long as Store alerts are on.
  • How to stop it: turn off Store alerts in the app, or change the Location permission in your phone’s settings at any time.

4. Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you scan a card’s barcode or QR code. If you import a picture of a card, the image you choose is read on your device to find the code. Camera frames and images are not saved by TapPass and are not uploaded.

5. Notifications

Notifications are used for store alerts and for the persistent “watching” notification required by Android while Store alerts are on. You can turn them off in your phone’s settings.

6. Purchases (TapPass Pro)

Subscriptions, including the free trial, are sold and processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. The app checks with Google Play whether your subscription is active. Google’s handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

7. What we do not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information, including for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • We do not know what you buy or how much you spend.

9. Legal bases (UK and EU users)

Where the UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for location (you can withdraw it at any time by turning off Store alerts or the permission), and on performance of a contract for providing TapPass Pro after purchase.

10. Your rights

Because your cards, list and history stay on your device, you control them directly: you can view, change or delete them in the app at any time. Depending on where you live (for example the UK, the EU, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may also have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object or restrict processing, and to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO). To make a request, email us at cubemobilegame@gmail.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

11. Data retention and security

Data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not keep copies. Requests made to the map-data service are handled under that service’s own policies.

12. Children

TapPass is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add features. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date, and for important changes we will also let you know in the app.
